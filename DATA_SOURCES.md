| Actuator constants | Ben Katz, motor-controller firmware `bgkatz/motorcontrol` (MIT licence), commit `93bbc60676ba4c2c0c64466c2b921eeb9d9fb1df` (2026-08-11): https://github.com/bgkatz/motorcontrol/blob/93bbc60676ba4c2c0c64466c2b921eeb9d9fb1df/Core/Inc/user_config.h | **Does not give gear 6.0 or KT_OUT 0.45.** `GR` and `KT` are runtime flash settings (`Core/Inc/user_config.h` L23, L26; set over serial, `Core/Src/fsm.c` L333-338) with empty-flash defaults `GR = 1.0`, `KT = 1.0` (`Core/Src/main.c` L203-204). Defaults also given there: pole pairs 21 (`main.c` L202), current limit 40 A (`main.c` L193); `Core/Inc/hw_config.h`: L_D = L_Q = 40 uH (L53-54), bus soft limit 40 V, hard overvoltage fault 43 V (L49-50). These are defaults of Katz's newer controller hardware (bgkatz/3phase_integrated), not a Mini Cheetah configuration. |
